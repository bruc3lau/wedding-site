The terms and conditions for hosting a wedding at our resort are as follows:

For Events at Layan Chapel:
o   A minimum booking of 10 rooms for 2 consecutive nights is required.

o  From 1 December to the end of February, a minimum stay of 4 consecutive nights per room is required.

o   During the Peak Season period (20 December – 20 January), a minimum stay of 5 consecutive nights per room applies.

o   A one-time group dinner on the wedding day is required, with a minimum spend of THB 3,500++ per guest.

o   A venue rental fee of THB 60,000++ per day will apply should you choose to engage an external wedding planner.

o   The venue rental fee will be waived when selecting one of our Resort Wedding Packages.

 

For Events at Layan Residences by Anantara:
o   A minimum stay of 2 consecutive nights at the residence is required, increasing to 4 consecutive nights for stays between December and February.

o   During the Peak Season period (20 December – 20 January), a minimum stay of 5 consecutive nights per residence applies.

o   A one-time group dinner on the wedding day is mandatory, with a minimum spend of THB 3,500++ per guest.
