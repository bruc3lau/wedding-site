#### 天气与雨天备案

Our main Green Lawn can accommodate up to 120 guests for dinner under favorable weather conditions. However, please note that our indoor function room can accommodate a maximum of approximately 70 guests for a dinner reception, depending on the final setup and decoration.

For the wedding ceremony, which typically lasts around 30-40 minutes, in the event of rain, we may either wait for the rain to pass or proceed with the ceremony using clear umbrellas. This allows the ceremony to continue while maintaining visibility and photography opportunities.

For larger wedding groups, or if you would prefer a more comprehensive weather backup solution, we recommend considering a transparent marquee for the dinner reception. The setup and installation cost is typically approximately THB 400,000–500,000 for a two-day rental, depending on the decoration, layout, and final number of guests.
